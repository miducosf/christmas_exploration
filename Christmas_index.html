FOR THE COZY DAYS

Find the fireplace first.

A cottage or casita with an actual kitchen, comfortable seating and a fireplace may matter more than the hotel star rating. Check whether the fireplace is usable and whether there's a proper oven for baking.

FOR THE WILDLIFE LOVER

One big animal moment.

Build the holiday around one special encounter—reindeer, giraffes, raptors or sea otters—and leave room for slower discoveries. Not every day needs an excursion.

Mind December 25

Holiday lights, zoos and restaurants may close or use special hours. The Living Desert is explicitly closed on Christmas Day 2026.

🛋️

Leave room to linger

Choose a place you’d actually enjoy staying inside. Baking together and sitting by the fire are part of the vacation, not backup plans.
